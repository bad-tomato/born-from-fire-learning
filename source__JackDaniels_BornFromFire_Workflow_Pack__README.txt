JACK DANIEL'S "BORN FROM FIRE" WORKFLOW PACK
Created and directed by Calixte LeBlanc, Crossroad Media
Full step-by-step breakdown: https://crossroadmedia.ca/jack (Workflows section)

This is the real production material from my Jack Daniel's spec ad, "Born in
Fire. Finished in Patience." Not a cleaned-up demo. The actual references,
keyframes, storyboard, grained plates, prompts and end cards, including the
scrapped human pour and the two failed full-length runs, so you can see what
works and what does not.

WHAT'S IN HERE

01-References
  The clean Old No. 7 bottle photo (label sharp, attached to every bottle
  generation) and the locked master distiller portrait. The distiller was
  generated in Midjourney V8 from a brief written by a panel of three Claude
  writers judged by a DP persona; a GPT Image 2 alternate is in 02-Keyframes.

02-Keyframes
  The five beats as stills, generated with GPT Image 2 at 2K: the char barrel
  interior, the charcoal drip, the ice drop, the match-cut glass opening, and
  the bottle hero plate (with the real label baked in from the reference).

03-Storyboard
  The one-sheet board for the 5-beat spine: FIRE, CHAR, PATIENCE, AMBER, HERO.

04-Grained-Plates
  THE ACTUAL VIDEO INPUTS. Every keyframe ran through a local 35mm film-grain
  pass before animation to kill the over-sharp AI look. These five plates are
  what Seedance actually received.

05-End-Cards
  "BORN IN FIRE. FINISHED IN PATIENCE." as transparent PNGs in gold serif
  (Hoefler Text, Didot, Cochin, plus split layouts). Real type, not AI text.

06-Prompts
  Every prompt in firing order with model, settings and references at the top.
  The files marked FAILED and SCRAPPED are kept on purpose. The two that
  matter most: the final 15-second master (shots 1 to 3 came from it) and the
  10-second Ice Drop that became shots 4 and 5.

07-Grain-Script
  grain.py, the exact Python (PIL) script that grained the plates: soft-light
  Gaussian noise plus a slight blur. Run it on your own keyframes.

08-Claude-Skills
  The Motion Design flow and the Seedance Director prompt author. Add them to
  Claude and describe your scene.

HOW TO RECREATE

1. Lock your character and product references first. Match the character's
   facing direction to your shot blocking, the model will not flip it for you.
2. Generate the five beats as stills and get each one approved before video.
3. Grain everything with grain.py. Animate from the grained plates.
4. Fire the 15-second master with all five plates attached. If a beat with
   humans handling the product comes back waxy, cut the hands and let the
   product do the acting (that is how the Ice Drop was born).
5. Stitch in your editor, score it with your own track, drop the gold type on
   the clean hero frame.

Swap in your own bottle and make it yours.
